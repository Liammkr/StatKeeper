Frequently asked questions

How do I log a shot?
  1. Open a team and select a game (or tap New Game).
  2. Tap a player from the home or opponent roster.
  3. Tap the spot on the court where the shot was taken, then choose Make or Miss.

StatKeeper works out whether it was a 2 or 3 from where you tapped.

What do the shot colors mean?
  • Purple: a made shot by the home team
  • Pink: a made shot by the opponent
  • Grey: a missed shot

Use Home, Both and Away to filter the court. Select Overall to see shots from every game.

How do I import a roster from MaxPreps?

Tap the import icon next to Home Team (or next to the opponent's roster), then paste the team's MaxPreps link, for example maxpreps.com/ca/city/school-name/basketball. StatKeeper adds each jersey number to the roster. If this season's roster isn't posted yet, last season's is used.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works without signing in, and your stats are saved on your device. Signing in with Google or Apple keeps your teams in sync between the app and the website.

Why don't I see my website teams in the app?

Make sure you sign in with the same account you use on the website. Signing in with Apple creates a separate account from Google, so a Google-based website account won't appear if you sign in with Apple.

How do I back up or export my stats?

In the app, go to Account → Export Data. This saves a file with all your teams, games and shots that you can store in Files, AirDrop or email.

How do I delete my account or data?

In the app, go to Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and all its stats. If you're not signed in, Delete All Data clears the stats saved on your device. You can also email us to request deletion.
